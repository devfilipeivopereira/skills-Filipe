# Generate Jordan Peterson Talks

## Overview

Generate talks with Jordan Peterson's authentic clinical psychology and depth-philosophy methodology: open with the Existential Provocation — suffering is intrinsic to Being, not a problem to be solved but a reality to be confronted, and the question is how to bear it with dignity; establish Chaos and Order as the fundamental map of human experience; present the Lobster and the Dominance Hierarchy as evidence that hierarchies are ancient, biological, and real — not social constructs; introduce the Hero's Journey as a psychological fact — the person who voluntarily confronts chaos and extracts meaning from it is not a literary character but the description of a psychologically functional human being; demand Shadow Integration as the prerequisite for genuine virtue — you are not good because you cannot be dangerous, but only if you could be and choose otherwise; deliver the Meaning-over-Happiness reframe — the goal is not happiness but the voluntary assumption of meaningful responsibility; teach Clean Your Room as the smallest possible act of order-making and the starting point of all genuine change; establish Tell the Truth as the foundational discipline from which all other virtues grow; present Compare Yourself to Who You Were Yesterday as the only valid growth metric; and close with the Voluntary Burden — a specific responsibility the audience will voluntarily assume before leaving. The default deliverable is a formatted Microsoft Word document.

Read [references/jordan_peterson_method.md](references/jordan_peterson_method.md) when writing or reviewing the talk. Treat that file as the source of truth for structure, timing, tone, checkpoints, and required output sections.
Use [scripts/create_talk_docx.py](scripts/create_talk_docx.py) to turn the final manuscript into a `.docx` file and enforce minimum word count before delivery.
Treat [scripts/core.py](scripts/core.py) as the portable logic layer, [scripts/adapters.py](scripts/adapters.py) as the environment adapter layer, and [scripts/config.json](scripts/config.json) as runtime defaults.

## Method Fidelity Rules

- The linked reference file is the governing specification for the Jordan Peterson methodology.
- Follow every non-negotiable in that reference exactly, even when this `SKILL.md` summarizes the method more briefly.
- If this `SKILL.md` and the reference file ever differ, the reference file wins.
- Do not flatten Peterson into a generic self-help talk. Preserve the existential weight of the opening, the Chaos-Order framework, the Lobster argument with its exact biological grounding, the Hero's Journey as psychological fact, the Shadow Integration imperative, the Meaning-over-Happiness reframe, the clinical precision of the Rules, and the Voluntary Burden close.
- Peterson is the deepest philosophical communicator in this skill library. His talks operate simultaneously at the levels of evolutionary biology, clinical psychology, Jungian depth psychology, literary analysis, and existential philosophy. Any manuscript that sounds like a self-help checklist or motivational speech has failed the fidelity test.
- Peterson's emotional register is distinctive and mandatory: he thinks out loud, he pauses when moved by his own argument, he sometimes becomes visibly emotional when discussing suffering or the capacity for human evil. This gravity must be present throughout the manuscript — it is not performance, it is the mark of someone who genuinely believes that what he is saying is of ultimate importance.
- Peterson never simplifies. He complexifies. He is the only speaker in this skill library who regularly says "it's more complicated than that" even when teaching what appears to be simple principles.
- Peterson's vocabulary is specific and must appear: "Being," "Chaos," "Order," "the Logos," "the Shadow," "the Hero," "the Dragon," "voluntary," "responsibility," "meaning," "expedient," "resentment," "tyranny," "competence hierarchy," "the unknown," "the explored territory," "tell the truth," "clean your room," "set your house in order."
- Controversial note: Peterson's work is genuinely controversial on political and social dimensions. The skill must not import his political positions into the talk. The methodology being reproduced is his psychological and philosophical framework for personal responsibility, meaning, and growth — which is broadly applicable and widely respected across political lines. Keep the manuscript focused on the psychological and philosophical content, not on the political dimensions.
- If the user asks for Jordan Peterson, keep the methodology intact unless the user explicitly asks to depart from it.

## Research Requirement

Before drafting the talk, browse the internet for:
- Current neuroscience or evolutionary biology findings relevant to the theme (dominance hierarchies, serotonin, the brain's processing of known vs. unknown).
- Recent examples from literature, mythology, or clinical psychology that illustrate the specific talk's theme.
- Peterson's most recent publications, lectures, or podcast content that may be relevant to the theme (*We Who Wrestle with God*, 2024).

## Preparation Rules

- Identify the specific form of chaos the audience is facing before building any section — the specific unknown they are avoiding, the specific responsibility they are refusing.
- Answer four questions first:
  - What is the specific chaos threatening this audience — and what is the order they must build in response?
  - What is the specific dragon they need to voluntarily confront?
  - What shadow dimension are they refusing to acknowledge?
  - What is the one voluntary burden they will leave with?
- Write the Central Existential Proposition before any section: the single statement that names the audience's condition and the path through it.
- The Central Existential Proposition must be weighty, not cheerful. It must acknowledge suffering before it offers orientation.
- Design the Voluntary Burden close as a specific, named responsibility — not a mindset shift, not an aspiration, but an act of deliberate assumption of a burden the audience has been avoiding.
- If the request is for a complete talk and no shorter limit is given, enforce at least 7,500 words.

## Style Rules

- Grave, earnest, intellectually dense — Peterson thinks out loud and the manuscript must capture the movement of his thought, not just its conclusions.
- Simultaneously scientific and mythological — every major point must have both a biological/psychological anchor and a mythological or literary illustration.
- Genuinely complex — do not simplify. Peterson's deepest insights feel simple when stated but require considerable unpacking. The unpacking is the value.
- Emotionally intense without being performative — Peterson's emotional moments arise from his genuine conviction that what he is saying is of life-and-death importance. This gravity must be present throughout.
- Use Dostoevsky, Solzhenitsyn, Nietzsche, Jung, biblical stories, and evolutionary biology as intellectual resources — not as name-drops but as genuine argument.
- Peterson's favorite rhetorical structure: "Here is the naive view. Here is why the naive view is wrong. Here is the more accurate and difficult view. Here are the consequences of accepting the difficult view."
- Never use motivational-speaker energy. Never use cheerful encouragement. Peterson's version of care is to tell you the hardest truth he knows about your situation.
- The close is a weighty invitation, not an emotional peak. The audience leaves with more responsibility than they arrived with — and that is the point.

## Quality Check

- Verify the opening names suffering as intrinsic to existence — not a problem to be solved.
- Verify Chaos and Order are both presented as necessary — too much of either is destructive.
- Verify the Lobster argument includes the serotonin mechanism and the 350-million-year evolutionary timeline.
- Verify the Hero's Journey is presented as a psychological fact, not a literary device.
- Verify Shadow Integration includes the specific claim: you are not good because you cannot be dangerous, but only if you could be and choose not to.
- Verify Meaning-over-Happiness explicitly rejects happiness as a valid life aim and replaces it with meaningful responsibility.
- Verify Clean Your Room is presented as the smallest possible starting point — immediately actionable, not metaphorical.
- Verify Tell the Truth includes the principle of precision of speech and the claim that lying corrupts the speaker as much as it deceives the listener.
- Verify Compare Yourself to Who You Were Yesterday explicitly rejects comparison with others as a standard.
- Verify the Voluntary Burden is a named, specific responsibility — not an aspiration.
- Verify at least two literary or mythological anchors (Dostoevsky, Solzhenitsyn, biblical stories, etc.) appear.
- Verify at least one clinical psychology reference (Jungian concept, personality research, clinical observation) grounds the argument.
- Verify the emotional gravity is present throughout — this is not a cheerful talk.
- Verify the generated `.docx` meets the minimum word count.
