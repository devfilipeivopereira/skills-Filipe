# Integrated Sermon Method

Use this file as the governing reference when generating sermons in the integrated six-layer system.

## Layer 1: Andy Stanley

Govern the sermon architecture through:

- ME
- WE
- GOD
- YOU
- WE FINAL

Require:

- one point only
- one Bottom Line
- one clear action

## Layer 2: Carmine Gallo

Govern communication quality through:

- radical clarity
- rule of three
- memorable phrasing
- one S.T.A.R. moment
- strong storytelling and images

## Layer 3: Steven Furtick

Govern delivery feel through:

- a title with wordplay from the text
- emotional pacing
- at least four participatory moments
- a perspective reframe

## Layer 4: John MacArthur

Govern the `GOD` section through:

- context
- authorial intent
- original-language precision
- Scripture interpreting Scripture
- doctrinal clarity

## Layer 5: Joseph Prince

Govern the tone of application through:

- grace before law
- identity before obligation
- Christ's finished work before effort

## Layer 6: Jim Edwards

Govern the opening through:

- pain-first connection
- emotional precision
- curiosity before explanation
- immediate relevance

## Integration Rules

- No layer cancels the others.
- `GOD` must remain exegetically serious.
- `YOU` must remain grace-based.
- `ME` and `WE` must create real felt tension before the text answers it.
- `WE FINAL` must cast a concrete shared vision, not merely summarize.

## Required Outputs

The sermon must include:

- title with wordplay
- short description
- 12 declared pre-sermon elements
- ME-WE-GOD-YOU-WE structure
- 4 participatory moments minimum
- 4 subpoints in `GOD`, each beginning with a verb
- one S.T.A.R. moment
- final memorable line
- closing prayer or invitation
- final audit report

Before any sermon paragraph, explicitly declare all 12 pre-sermon elements required by the method.

In the `GOD` section, include 4 subpoints that each:

- begin with a verb
- emerge from the text
- include one illustration
- include three practical applications
- include one concrete teaching suggestion

The final audit report must include both:

- a score table from 0 to 2 across the required criteria
- a checklist report with `Ok` or `Revisar`

## Hard Restrictions

- no em dash
- no mention of storms or foundations
- no use or allusion to the house on rock and sand unless explicitly requested

## Final Diagnostic

Before finishing, verify:

- one point governs all sections
- the Bottom Line is memorable and repeated
- the opening names real pain
- the `GOD` section is text-faithful
- the `YOU` section is grace-first
- the final line is memorable
- the audit report is included
