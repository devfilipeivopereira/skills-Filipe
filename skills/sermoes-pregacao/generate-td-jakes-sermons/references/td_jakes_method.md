# T.D. Jakes Sermon Method

Use this reference when the user wants a sermon built around T.D. Jakes's documented preaching method, with personal feeding in the text, narrative humanization, extended metaphor, and prophetic proclamation.

## Foundational Philosophy

Jakes does not go first to the text merely to build a sermon. He goes to be fed, and from that abundance he feeds others.

This means:

- the sermon begins with genuine encounter
- vulnerability is substance, not strategy
- authority grows from lived text, not performance alone

## Four Preparation Steps

### 1. Study Yourself Full

- read the text hungrily
- let it address God, humanity, and the preacher personally
- identify the passage's pivot moments, like GPS direction changes

### 2. Think Yourself Clear

Before structuring the sermon, answer:

- what question does this text raise or resolve?
- who is the central person?
- what is at stake?
- what is the problem?
- what prescription does God provide?

Use:

- person
- problem
- prescription

### 3. Pray Yourself Hot

The message must matter deeply before it can be proclaimed powerfully.

Cold correctness is not enough.

### 4. Let Yourself Go

When preaching time comes, drop inhibition and let the delivery rise naturally into proclamation.

## Narrative Framework

### Person

Humanize the biblical character until the audience sees itself in that person.

Focus on:

- emotional state
- hidden shame
- delay
- rejection
- abandonment
- confusion

### Problem

The problem has three layers:

1. visible circumstance
2. emotional impact
3. theological meaning it seems to imply

### Prescription

The prescription is not advice. It is God's answer.

It typically includes:

1. reframing
2. process
3. proclamation

## Extended Metaphor

Jakes often uses one large metaphor as the sermon's interpretive engine.

Examples:

- grape to wine
- seed to tree
- olive to oil

The metaphor is not a decorative illustration. It interprets the suffering.

## Full Sermon Flow

### Opening: Humanization

- narrate the biblical scene vividly
- collapse the distance between text and listener
- name the emotional struggle with precision

### Body: Exegesis and Reframing of Pain

- follow the GPS turns in the text
- expose emotional and theological pressure
- unfold the central metaphor
- show God's perspective inside the process

### Climax: Proclamation of Destiny

- move from explanation to declaration
- transfer the truth from text to listener
- let the rhythm rise naturally
- end in prophetic blessing and destiny language

## Governing Principles

- exegesis first, therapy second
- vulnerability is real life, not technique
- never skip person, problem, or prescription
- metaphor interprets rather than decorates
- crescendo is released, not manufactured
- preach to people, not at them
