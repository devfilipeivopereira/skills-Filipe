# Joel Osteen Preaching Method

Use this file as the governing reference when generating sermons in Joel Osteen's style.

## Core Conviction

Every sermon should leave the hearer better than they came in:

- more hopeful
- more faith-filled
- more expectant

The sermon must not end in shame, complexity, or obligation.

## Structural Marks

### 1. Humorous Opening

- short
- clean
- universal
- disarming

The purpose is to lower defenses and create a safe atmosphere.

### 2. Exact Scripture Confession

Use the standard text exactly:

"Esta é a minha Bíblia. Eu sou o que ela diz que eu sou. Eu tenho o que ela diz que eu tenho. Eu posso fazer o que ela diz que eu posso fazer. Hoje eu serei ensinado pela Palavra de Deus. Declaro com ousadia: minha mente está alerta, meu coração está receptivo. Nunca serei o mesmo. Em nome de Jesus. Deus abençoe vocês."

### 3. Short Anchor Text

Choose one to three verses with clear promise, identity, favor, blessing, or victory potential.

### 4. Clear Theme Statement

The theme should be:

- positive
- direct
- immediately understandable

### 5. Three Core Cycles

Each cycle should contain:

- a concrete story
- a memorable principle
- a biblical story or text retexturized for today

The three cycles should grow in depth and emotional force.

## Limiting Narrative

Each sermon should identify one inner negative storyline such as:

- I am not enough
- it is too late for me
- nothing good is coming
- my giant is too big

The sermon replaces that narrative with spoken faith.

## Spoken Declarations

Collective declarations are not optional. They are part of how mindset is transformed.

### Characteristics

- first person singular
- brief
- faith-filled
- biblically anchored
- repeatable aloud

## Tone

- conversational
- smiling
- hopeful
- pastoral
- never condemning

The hearer should feel welcomed, not attacked.

## Conclusion

The ending should include:

1. hopeful summary
2. prophetic declaration over the congregation
3. invitation for salvation or recommitment
4. the standard salvation prayer

## Standard Salvation Prayer

"Senhor Jesus, me arrependo dos meus pecados. Acredito que Você morreu e ressuscitou por mim. Venha para a minha vida. Quero te servir todos os dias do resto da minha vida. Em nome de Jesus, Amém."

## Final Diagnostic

Before finishing, verify:

- the sermon is plain enough for an ordinary hearer
- the hearer leaves with more hope, not more burden
- the main point is repeated with variation
- the declarations are explicit
- the conclusion blesses, invites, and celebrates response
