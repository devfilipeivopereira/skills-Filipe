# Eugene Lowry Narrative Preaching Method

Use this file as the governing reference when generating sermons in Eugene Lowry's style.

## Core Conviction

The sermon exists in time, not in space. Therefore its sequence is part of its meaning. To change the order is to preach a different sermon.

The sermon should move like a narrative or a jazz progression:

- tension
- complication
- reversal
- Gospel experience
- opened future

## The Lowry Loop

### Stage 1: Upsetting the Equilibrium

- create a sensed discrepancy
- do not announce the destination
- disturb the hearer's settled view

The hearer must want to know how this can possibly resolve.

### Stage 2: Analyzing the Discrepancy

- deepen the problem
- show why the obvious answer fails
- let the hearer feel genuinely stuck

This stage must be substantial. If the conflict is shallow, the Gospel reversal will feel shallow.

### Stage 3: Disclosing the Clue to Resolution

- introduce the `Aha!`
- reveal the clue as something that comes from outside human resourcefulness
- make the reversal genuinely surprising and Gospel-shaped

This is not "try harder." This is the turn created by what God has done or is doing.

### Stage 4: Experiencing the Gospel

- dwell in the Good News
- let the hearer inhabit grace, not just define it
- narrate or concretize the Gospel's force in relation to the opening bind

### Stage 5: Anticipating the Consequences

- open possibilities
- widen the horizon of what becomes possible because of the Gospel
- avoid converting this into a checklist

The sermon may end with image, question, or possibility rather than a fully closed system.

## Gospel Reversal vs. Moralism

False turn:

- "You need more effort."
- "You just have to believe harder."

True Gospel turn:

- God has acted.
- God has given what the bind could never generate.
- because of that gift, new possibilities open

## Language for the Ear

Write for listeners, not readers.

- short sentences
- rhythmic variation
- audible images
- implied pauses

The sermon should sound speakable and hearable.

## The Bind and the Itch

The opening discrepancy must be lived and felt.

Examples:

- wanting to forgive and being unable
- praying and hearing silence
- loving and feeling repeatedly wounded

The bind is not a classroom puzzle. It is a human tension with no obvious exit.

## Final Diagnostic

Before finishing, verify:

- the opening does not disclose the end
- the problem becomes deeper, not merely longer
- the reversal comes from the Gospel and surprises the hearer
- the Good News is experienced, not merely explained
- the ending offers possibility rather than obligation
